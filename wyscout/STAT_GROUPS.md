# Stat groups

_Generated by `python -m wyscout.derived --write-groups` from `BASE_GROUPS` and the derived-stat registry in `wyscout/derived.py` - edit those, not this file._

Stats are organised into functional groups for team and player analysis. Each group lists

1. **Raw stats** - stems from the Wyscout data. A stem such as `key_passes` expands to `total_key_passes`, `average_key_passes` (per exposure) and `percent_key_passes` wherever those columns exist.
2. **Engineered stats** - built in `wyscout/derived.py` (details in [DERIVED_STATS.md](DERIVED_STATS.md)). Column prefixes: `total_` summed count, `average_` per exposure (per match for opponent-based stats), `percent_` 0-100 rate, `ratio_` plain ratio.

A stat may belong to more than one group on purpose (e.g. `progressive_run` is both Progression and Dribbling, `head_shots` is both Shooting and Aerial). Scope is `team`, `player` or `both`.
